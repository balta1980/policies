Privacy Policy for Scorer

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the Scorer mobile application (herein referred to as "the Application"). The Application is designed to help users track scores for card and domino games.

2. Data Collection and Use

While the core scoring functionality of the Application does not collect or transmit personal information, we use third-party services that may collect device-specific data to serve advertisements.

  • Personal Data: We (the developers) do not directly ask for names, emails, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Persistent Data: The Application uses local storage on your device to keep track of game scores and settings (persistent variables). This data is stored locally and stays on your device at all times.

3. Permissions

The Application requires standard internet access to serve banner advertisements. However, it does not require or request any special privacy-sensitive device permissions, such as access to your precise location, camera, contacts, or microphone.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

To keep the Application free, we use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google LLC.

Google and its advertising partners may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and cookies to serve ads based on your usage of the Application and other apps on your device. These third-party services collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • You can learn more about how Google uses data for advertising by visiting the Google Partner Sites Privacy Policy.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your Android device settings (usually found under Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization) or by visiting Google's My Ad Center.

5. Data Retention

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the Application's cache or uninstalling the Application from your device.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not intentionally target children under the age of 13. While the scoring app itself is safe for all ages, the third-party advertising network (Google AdMob) may collect data to serve ads. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information or is receiving inappropriate ads, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect user information, we cannot notify you personally; therefore, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
