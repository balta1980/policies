1. Nature of the Application

It is expressly stated that Chronav is not, nor does it claim to be, a certified chronograph or stopwatch. The application is not homologated under any national or international metrology standards (such as ISO standards applicable to measuring instruments), and its code and operation are not subject to being audited, calibrated, or endorsed by official standardization bodies.

3. Hardware Technical Limitations

The times calculated and recorded by Chronav depend intrinsically on the user's mobile device hardware (processor, memory) and the latency of the operating system (Android/iOS). Given that smartphones are not dedicated precision instruments and manage multiple background processes, it is impossible to guarantee millisecond-level accuracy consistently and auditably.

4. Usage Restrictions (Critical Environments)

Consequently, the data and times generated by Chronav must be considered strictly as approximations or estimates for internal use. The use of this application is strictly discouraged in:

  • Any environment, process, or legal dispute where the exact timing of an event is critical or could result in safety, financial, or legal consequences.
